3. Health data

Your weight, goal weight, activity and similar information is health data. The GDPR calls it “special category data” and POPIA calls it “special personal information”. It gets extra protection.

How we handle it

  • We process it only with your explicit consent, which the app asks for before you enter it.
  • We use it only to run the features you see, such as progress, targets and streaks.
  • We never use it for advertising, never send it to advertising partners, and never sell it.
  • It is never shown to other users.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the data (for example by editing your profile) or by deleting your account (see how to delete your account). Withdrawing does not affect anything we did lawfully before. Without this data, features such as progress tracking cannot work.

4. Ads and your choices

Banting is free with ads from Google AdMob. Ads are shown to free users only, and never in onboarding, cooking mode, Profile, the meal plan or the shopping list.

  • Consent form. Where required, the first time you open the app we show Google’s consent form (the User Messaging Platform). You choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Personalised ads use your advertising ID and your activity across apps to show ads you may find more relevant.
  • Non-personalised ads are based on general information such as the app you are using and your rough location, not your history. You will still see ads, but they are less tailored.
  • Never based on health data. We never use your weight, goals or any other health information to target ads, and we do not share it with AdMob.
  • Facebook sign-in is set up so that Meta’s tools do not collect your advertising ID and do not log automatic app events.

You can change your choice at any time where the app offers it, and in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads) where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation. You can also buy “remove ads” (see below). Google explains its own use of data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Purchases

The optional one-time “remove ads” purchase is handled by Google Play Billing, and managed through RevenueCat, which tells the app whether you have bought it. Google handles your payment. We do not see or store your card or bank details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous or account-linked user ID and your purchase status, and Google shares purchase details with them.

6. Who we share data with

We use trusted companies (called “processors”) to run the app. They may only use your data to provide their service to us, under written agreements, unless stated otherwise.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in, and storing your profile and tracking data.
  • Google AdMob: showing and measuring ads. Google acts as an independent controller for its own advertising purposes.
  • Google Play: distributing the app and processing purchases.
  • RevenueCat: managing the “remove ads” purchase.
  • Meta (Facebook): only if you choose to sign in with Facebook.
  • Expo: delivering the app and its updates.
  • A crash reporting service: receiving crash and technical reports.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or if the app is sold or transferred to someone else (they would have to respect this policy).

7. International transfers

Our service providers store and process data in Google Cloud data centres and elsewhere, which may be outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area and outside South Africa. We run Banting from North Macedonia.

When data is transferred, we make sure it stays protected, for example by using providers covered by an adequacy decision or by using standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, and other safeguards that POPIA allows.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and app data: until you delete your account. Deletion is completed within 30 days, including from backups.
  • Purchase records: held by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as the law requires.
  • Crash reports and advertising data: kept for the limited periods set by those services.
  • Emails to us: only as long as needed to deal with your request.

See Delete your account for details.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including encrypted connections, access controls so you can only reach your own data, and secure providers. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the regulators as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and POPIA you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct anything that is wrong (you can edit most details yourself in Profile);
  • Delete your data (see Delete your account);
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing (we do not send marketing);
  • Restrict processing in some situations;
  • Data portability: ask for the data you gave us in a common, machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example for health data or personalised ads; and
  • Complain to a regulator.

To use a right, email fitnfoods@gmail.com from your account email address. We will reply within 30 days and normally do not charge a fee. We may need to confirm who you are first.

Complaints

We would like the chance to fix things first, but you can complain at any time:

  • South Africa: the Information Regulator (South Africa), inforeg.org.za.
  • North Macedonia: the Personal Data Protection Agency, dzlp.mk.
  • European Union: your local data protection authority. You can find yours on the European Data Protection Board website.

11. Children

Banting is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. Please check back now and then.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email fitnfoods@gmail.com, or see our contact page.
