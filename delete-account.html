Delete your Banting account

You can delete your account and data at any time. This page is for the Banting app, run by Magdalena Ivanovska.

Deleting is permanent

Once your account is deleted we cannot bring it back. If you only want to remove some information, you can edit your profile in the app instead.

Option 1: delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Banting and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Account section and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the message and confirm. For your security, the app may ask you to sign in again first.

Option 2: ask us by email (no app needed)

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, email us:

  1. Send an email to fitnfoods@gmail.com.
  2. Write it from the email address of your Banting account, so we know it is you.
  3. Use the subject Delete my Banting account.

Email a deletion request

If you signed up with Google or Facebook, write from the email address linked to that account. We may ask one question to confirm it is you. We will never ask for your password.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account we permanently delete:

  • your profile (name, email, age, gender, height, goal and activity level);
  • your weight, water and activity history, and your streaks;
  • your saved recipes and meal-plan choices;
  • your settings, including notification preferences; and
  • your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication.

How long it takes

Deleting in the app starts straight away. Everything is fully deleted, including from backups, within 30 days. For email requests, we confirm by email once your account has been deleted.

Good to know

  • Your ad-free purchase belongs to your Google Play account. Deleting your Banting account does not cancel or refund it. See the terms for refunds.
  • Google or Facebook: deleting your Banting account does not delete your Google or Facebook account. You can remove Banting from the connected apps in their settings.
