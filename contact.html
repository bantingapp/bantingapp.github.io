Contact us

Have a question, a problem, or a privacy request? We would love to help.

Email support

fitnfoods@gmail.com

We reply within 3 working days.

What to include

The more we know, the faster we can help. Please tell us:

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • The app version (you can find it at the bottom of the Profile tab).
  • A screenshot, if you can.
  • The email address of your Banting account, if your question is about your account.

Never send us your password. We will never ask for it.

Common requests

  • Deleting your account: see Delete your account.
  • Reporting a post or person: use More → Report post in Community, so our team sees it straight away. See the Community guidelines.
  • Privacy requests (access, correction, a copy of your data, objections): email us with the subject “Privacy request”. See the privacy policy.
  • Purchase problems: in the app, go to Profile and choose Restore purchase. Refunds for Google Play purchases follow Google Play’s policies. See the terms.
