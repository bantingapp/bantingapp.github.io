Community guidelines

Community is a place to share your Banting meals and get inspired. These simple rules keep it friendly for everyone.

The short version: be kind, keep it Banting, only post photos you took yourself, and never give medical advice.

1. Be kind

Cheer people on and keep comments friendly and supportive. Everyone is at a different point in their journey. Share what worked for you, and be gentle when someone is struggling.

Not allowed: insults, bullying, mocking someone’s body or progress, harassment, hate speech, or anything that targets people for who they are.

2. Keep it Banting

Share low-carb meals and tips, and only post photos you took yourself. Photos copied from websites, cookbooks or other people’s posts are not allowed, because they may belong to someone else.

When you post a meal, add a short caption, pick the meal type, and link the recipe if it comes from the app.

3. No medical claims

Share what works for you, without telling others what to do about their health. Please do not:

  • promise cures or results (“this will reverse your diabetes”);
  • advise anyone to stop or change medication, such as insulin;
  • give diagnoses or treatment advice.

If someone asks a health question, the kindest answer is “speak to your doctor”.

Also not allowed

  • Spam and advertising: no promotions, links to shops or services, referral codes or repeated posts.
  • Nudity and sexual content, and graphic or violent images.
  • Personal information: do not share your own or anyone else’s phone number, address, email or other private details. Please also avoid photos that show other people, especially children, without their permission.
  • Illegal content, or anything that breaks someone else’s rights.
  • Pretending to be someone else.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a post: tap the three dots (More) on the post and choose Report post. Our team will take a look. We cannot always tell you the outcome, but every report is read.
  • Block someone: tap More on one of their posts and choose Hide posts from that person. Their posts disappear from your feed.
  • Delete your own post: tap More on your post and choose Delete post.

If something is urgent or you need to tell us more, email fitnfoods@gmail.com.

What happens when the rules are broken

We look at what happened and respond fairly. Depending on how serious it is, we may:

  • remove the post or comment;
  • send a friendly warning;
  • temporarily stop the account from posting; or
  • close the account for serious or repeated breaches, as set out in our terms of use.

We may use automated checks as well as human review to find content that breaks these rules. If you think we made a mistake, email us and we will look again.

By posting, you also agree to the Community section of our terms. How we handle your posts and data is explained in our privacy policy.
